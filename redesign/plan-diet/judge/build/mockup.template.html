Plan, Gym and Diet: the merged pick

Two builders made the new Plan, Gym and Diet tabs. I tested both and merged the best of each. Every screen shows OLD (the real app 0.4.1, screenshots) next to NEW (my merged pick), in light and dark.

Turn on Questions to see 13 numbered questions drawn on the screens. They are drawn on the light frames. One tap picks an answer; tap another to change it. Copy my picks copies all answers as text to paste into the Training chat. All numbers on the screens are made up.

In short

The pick

A mix. A (“fastest at the gym”) gives the Plan day, the edit rules and the muscle check. B (“see your body”) gives the body drawing, the opened exercise, the scanner and the gear for Settings. Both builders’ bugs are fixed in the merge.

Bugs found

  • B: Swap and Add can put the same exercise twice in a day (2 bugs, still in B’s latest version).
  • A: the plan’s own swap is hidden for the Romanian deadlift; the day arrows have no background; the Add sheet prints “null”.

Measured taps

  • See Thursday: 2 in both.
  • Swap an exercise: 3 from the day page in both.
  • Log a scanned food: 3 plus the scan in both.
  • Log a usual food again: 2 in both.

⚠️ Where I disagree with you

  • The muscle check only shows numbers, as you asked. But the numbers show real gaps (calves and back thighs 3 sets a week, abs 27). Question 74 asks if the plan should be reviewed.
  • Search by food name (answer 64) works badly in Open Food Facts: “skyr” gave French and Arabic products first. So scan and your own foods come first, search last (question 79).

Plan day

OLD: one long scroll of all 5 days. NEW: one day per page that reads like Today, opened on today, with the real sets of this week.

What NEW takes from whom

  • A: the header (“Week 3 · Build 1 ›”, big day name, TODAY), the meta line, the walk card first, pair headers in words, dense cards with plain muscle words, the day arrows at the bottom.
  • B: focus words and done ticks in the day strip (A took them too), the gear, the “Whole plan check” card at the end of the day.
  • Fixed: A’s arrow bar now has a solid frosted background, so cards no longer show through.
  • Moved: A’s “Muscles” chip goes to the end of the day (B’s place). The top row has no room for 4 buttons at 375 px.

Nothing lost

Day focus, walk text, sets × reps, rest and swaps are all still here. “A1 · 30 s to move” becomes “Pair A · back and forth · 3 rounds · rest 90 s after each round”. The “Swap:” line moves into the opened exercise (“The plan’s own swap”) and the Swap list (“Plan’s pick”). Print is gone, as you decided.

Not taken

B’s warm-up step on the day page (Today already shows it). B’s big day map: question 70.

Both builders’ muscle lists match the free exercise database exactly for all 27 plan exercises. The 9 swap-only exercises are each builder’s own reading and still need checking against the database (build step 1). Question 71 switches the drawing on the screen.

Same lines as 0.4.1 (phase, sets rule, RIR, walk note) and the RIR line. Both builders drew it this way. Another week shows “Showing week 7. This week is week 3.” with “Back to this week” on the day page.

Edit: swap, remove, add, back to the plan

Every edit saves at once and lasts for that day of the plan (your answer 65). Past and running workouts never change.

What NEW takes from whom

  • A: Edit mode with Swap and Remove on each card, “Add an exercise (2 of 2 left)”, “Done editing”; the green “Lasts” box; exercises already in the day are named but never offered; 8 s Undo; the “Changed by you · 17 Nov” card.
  • B: the plan’s own swaps are always offered and marked “Plan’s pick”; the confirm step on “Back to the plan’s Tuesday” (question 73).
  • Fixed: B’s two bugs (the same exercise twice in a day), A’s hidden plan swap (the Romanian deadlift now offers Cable pull-through as Plan’s pick), A’s “null” in the Add sheet.
  • Machines your first visit has not found stay hidden, with one line saying which (as 0.4.1’s “only if the gym has it”).

Not taken

B’s sets stepper on each card in Edit mode. You asked for add, remove and swap; changing sets touches the week rules.

Whole-plan muscle check

Weekly sets per muscle, Monday to Friday. It only shows numbers and never changes the plan. Numbers from research 5.3 (weeks 2 to 5).

What NEW takes from whom

  • A: “4 things stand out”, two numbers per row (direct · with helpers), helper-only cases in grey (“Under 10 direct; 10+ counting helpers”), lower back without a warning.
  • B: the shoulder parts as “Part of shoulders” rows, and “Not trained on purpose in this plan: traps, forearms, neck and inner thighs”.
  • Fixed: B called lower back “0 sets · Under 10”; B’s day map said “Hamstrings 6” while its check said 3.

The 3 gaps (research 5.4)

Back thighs: 3 direct sets a week, Tuesday only. Calves: 3, Friday only (2 in weeks 7 to 11). Abs: 27, about 3 times the 10-set floor. Question 74 is drawn next to them.

Gym: Machines and Exercises

Exercises and Machines leave the Plan tab and live in Gym behind one switch. The Machines list is the same as 0.4.1.

  • Machines: the 0.4.1 list and machine cards, word for word, under a Machines | Exercises switch (both builders). The Plan tab’s duplicate Machines part is gone.
  • Exercises: every exercise with “In the plan: Mon” or “Swap only”, a small map, tap = the same exercise layer with one map. Grouped by muscle (A) or A to Z with chips (B): question 75.
  • The gear for Settings is at the top right of every tab (B): question 67.

Diet day

A new tab (your answer 62). Today against your targets first, then the fast ways to add food. Targets come from your plan; the numbers here are made up.

What NEW takes from whom

  • A: the 4 ways row (Search, Digits, Quick add, Copy yesterday), “Log again · one tap” as a list with “had today”, the bottom bar “Your foods · Scan”.
  • B: the 7-day average line, carbs and fat (with rings), meals (question 76).
  • Not taken: B’s second Scan button (B had Scan in the row and again at the bottom).
  • OLD has no food log: Today only shows the targets (“Food a day”, “Protein a day”). They stay on Today.

Add food: scan, digits, search

Scan is the main way. Typing the digits is always offered. Search runs only when you tap Search. Only the barcode or the word is sent to Open Food Facts (your answer 64).

  • Scanner: B’s layer (a misread keeps the camera open: “That read looks wrong. Hold the phone still and try again.”), plus A’s “Looking it up · Only the barcode number is sent.” while it waits.
  • The scanner stays on one screen while the camera runs (iPhone can stop the camera on a page change). The camera view is dark in both looks, as both builders drew it.
  • Digits: both builders test the last digit before any lookup, so a typo costs no lookup.
  • Search: A’s sheet. One box takes a food name or barcode digits. Your foods come first and work offline.

Check before saving

Every food from Open Food Facts is checked by you before it is saved. Anyone can add products there, so numbers can be wrong.

  • A: the header text, the soft warning (“The pack size says 1 gram. That is likely wrong.”), the portion starting at the pack’s serving (43.1 g).
  • B: the 4 fields in a grid, “The numbers add up.”, the meal row (if question 76 is b).
  • Fixed: B started every new food at 100 g (Nutella: 539 kcal by default).
  • The Open Food Facts credit line is on every sheet that shows their data and at the foot of Diet.
  • Question 78 (where the saved food syncs) is drawn on “Save and add”.

Tab bar and Settings

Diet would be a 6th tab. Both builders move Settings off the bar to a gear button, so the bar keeps 5 tabs.

  • OLD: Today · Progress · Plan · Gym · Settings. NEW follows your answer to question 67. The recommended answer: Settings is the gear at the top right of every tab (B; A had it on Today and Progress only), and Settings keeps its own page with a “‹ Today” back link.
  • The order follows question 68 on every NEW screen of this page. Questions 67 and 68 are drawn on the Gym and Plan screens.
