## 1. Words you need

Short list. The full list is in [plan v1, section 1](2026-11-16-training-plan.md) and [WORKFLOW.md, section 0](../WORKFLOW.md).

- **Rep**: one movement. **Set**: a group of reps without a break. "3 × 10" = 3 sets of 10 reps.
- **Pair (superset)**: two exercises for different muscles, one after the other, then a rest.
- **RIR (reps in reserve)**: how many more good reps you could still do when you stop. RIR 2 = stop with 2 left.
- **Warm-up set**: a light set before the real (working) sets.
- **Deload**: an easier week, so the body can recover and grow.
- **Smallest step**: the smallest weight jump on that exercise (one dumbbell size, one plate on a stack).
- **Trend weight**: the 7-day average of your weigh-ins.
- **Slope (incline)**: how steep the treadmill is, in %.

## 3. Your choice: the walk comes first

You chose to do the 30-minute fast uphill walk **before** the weights (intake question 35 b). This plan is built around that choice.

**Why it works.** Walking is gentle. The newest research finds that cardio done with weights does not reduce muscle growth; doing weights first gives only a little more strength, not more muscle. Walking is the mildest kind of cardio. So the walk first is fine for your goal, if we protect your legs on leg days.

**How the plan protects your legs and your lifts:**

```
 walk 30 min  (counts as warm-up)
   leg days: 2 % less slope,
   effort 4-5 of 10, not 5-6
        |
 2-3 min: water, heart rate settles
        |
 4 min lift warm-up (moves below)
        |
 weights: machines first,
   free weights after,
   calves last (walk tires them)
```

1. **Easier walk on leg days** (Tuesday, Friday): 2 % less slope than on the other days, effort 4 to 5 out of 10.
2. **Never hard before weights.** The walk is "talk in short sentences" (5 to 6 out of 10), never out of breath. If you finish the walk breathing hard, the next walk is 1 % less steep.
3. **A short break.** After the walk, drink water and give your heart rate 2 to 3 minutes to come down before the first heavy set.
4. **A short warm-up for the lifts** (4 minutes). The walk warms the body, but not the joints and the upper body. See section 4.
5. **Smart order on leg days.** Stable machines first (leg press, Smith machine with safety stops), free weights and balance moves later, light machines last. Calf raises come last, or not at all on Tuesday, because the uphill walk already works the calves hard.
6. **The walk counts.** It is in the calorie numbers (private plan), and it replaces the bike warm-up of the old layout.

## 8. Week 1: learn the moves and find your weights (WORKFLOW C)

Week 1 teaches each exercise and finds a good weight. Nobody tests a maximum lift.

**Rules for week 1:**
- 2 working sets per exercise, not 3.
- Stop every set with about 3 reps left. It will feel easy. That is on purpose.
- No progression yet. Normal walk (at the starting settings) and abs.
- Ask staff to show a machine if you are unsure. Write down the seat and pin settings.

**For each exercise:**
1. Learn: read the form cue, set the machine.
2. Warm-up: light weight, 10 reps.
3. Set 1: pick a weight. Do reps, stop with about 3 left. Note the reps and "how many more could I do?" (0-1 / 2-3 / 4-6 / 7+).
4. Pick the set 2 weight from this table:

| After set 1 | Set 2 |
|---|---|
| Reached the top of the range with 7 or more left | Add 2 steps |
| Reached the top with 4 to 6 left | Add 1 step |
| In the range with 2 to 3 left | Keep it. This is your starting weight. |
| In the range with 0 to 1 left | Remove 1 step |
| Could not reach the bottom of the range | Remove 1 to 2 steps |

5. Set 2: the weight that fits is your starting weight for week 2. If it is still off, week 2 fixes it with the normal rule (section 9).

**First visit check (open question 17):** note which extra machines exist (lat pulldown, seated row, leg curl, pec deck, calf raise) and the dumbbell steps (for example 2, 4, 6 kg or 2, 3, 4 kg).

**Before the first session (Monday of week 1, in the morning):** weigh in, measure, take photos (section 11).

## 9. Progression: double progression (WORKFLOW E1)

First add reps inside the range, then add weight. One bad set never lowers the weight; only two bad sessions in a row do.

| Last session | Next session |
|---|---|
| All sets at the top of the range, good form, 1 or more reps left | Add the smallest step. Back to the bottom of the range. |
| Some sets in the range, not all at the top | Same weight. Aim for 1 more rep per set. |
| A set 1 to 2 reps below the range | Same weight. Check rest and form. |
| A set 3 or more reps below the range, two sessions in a row | Lower the weight about 10 % (to the next size the gym has). Restart at the bottom of the range. |
| Pain or form broke down | Lower the weight. No increase until form is good. |
| "How many more?" was 4+ on every set, at the top of the range (weeks 2 to 4 only) | Add 2 steps instead of 1. |
| The next step is a big jump (more than about 10 % heavier) | First reach 2 reps above the range at the old weight, then move up. |

**Smallest steps:** dumbbells: the next size (check the gym's steps, question 17); machines and cables: one plate on the stack; Smith squat and hip thrust: 2.5 kg each side.
**Bodyweight and timed abs:** when all sets reach the top, add 2 reps or 5 seconds.
**Walk:** two walks in a row at 5 out of 10 or easier → add 1 % slope (up to 12 %), then 0.2 km/h speed (up to 5 km/h; changed 2026-11-18 from about 6 km/h, see the [trainer weekly plan](2026-11-18-trainer-weekly-plan.md), decision D8). Leg days always stay 2 % below the other days. The "12-3-30" walk (12 % slope, about 4.8 km/h, 30 minutes) is a later target, not a start.

## 12. Food, weight and check-ins (WORKFLOW B4 and F)

You asked for a calorie target and a protein target (intake question 32 a). Your numbers are in the private plan.

**How the numbers are made (formulas only):**
- **Resting energy:** Mifflin-St Jeor formula, men: 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age + 5.
- **Maintenance:** resting energy × a daily-life number from intake question 30, plus the gym (walk and weights, averaged over 7 days). Checked against the classic "exercise 3 to 5 days a week" number (× 1.55). A first guess only; the weigh-in trend corrects it after about 3 weeks.
- **Weight goal:** you asked the app to suggest one (26 d). The B4 rule uses BMI and waist. The suggestion, its reasons and your other choice are in the private plan.
- **Daily calories:** maintenance minus 300 to 500 (cut), about maintenance (recomp), or plus about 10 % (lean bulk). Never under 1,500 a day.
- **Protein:** 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg (bulk or recomp), 2.0 to 2.4 g per kg (cut). If BMI is over 30, use the weight at BMI 25 for your height. Split over 3 to 5 meals.
- **Safe speed:** weight loss never faster than 1 % of body weight a week; gain never faster than 0.5 %.

**Weigh-ins (F1).** You have a scale at home (34 a): weigh every morning, after the toilet, before food and drink, in light clothes. If you miss days, at least 3 times a week. Only the 7-day average counts.

**Weekly check-in (F2), Monday before the workout:** workouts done (out of 5), new bests, walk minutes, trend weight change against your target band (private plan).

**What changes if the trend is off target (F3):** nothing in weeks 1 and 2. After that, only after **2 weeks in a row** off target, by the table in WORKFLOW F3 (steps of 150 calories a day, or 2,000 more steps a day). Your exact limits in kg are in the private plan. If lifts go down for 2 weeks while losing weight, eat a little more.

**Every 4 weeks (F4), Monday morning of weeks 1, 4, 8 and 12:**

- Photos (38 a): front, side and back, same light and place. Kept **only** in the phone's Photos app (an album called "Training"). Never synced, never in GitHub.
