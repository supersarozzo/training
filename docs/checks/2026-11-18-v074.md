### Double-tap guard

- Tests first: 3b1ebf6 (D1-D6), then review gaps e52f0e7 (D7) and 2064699 (D8).
- Finding on `touch-action` through `*`: it is a property of elements, never of text nodes. A tap on text lands on the element around it, which gets `manipulation` from `*`, and WebKit applies the value to all children. So the CSS covers text in theory. Why the iPhone still zoomed could not be shown here (no iPhone Safari on the computer; web reports are mixed). Sources: WebKit blog "More Responsive Tapping on iOS", the CSS touch-action spec.
- Browser check (Chrome, simulated touches on the walk sheet's "+"): three quick taps 15 → 16 → 17 → 18, a later single tap 19 (each tap once; the 2nd and 3rd cancelled and replayed); a double tap in the number box left alone; no console errors.

Minor findings fixed: no replay on a non-cancellable touchend, no replay on a button that left the screen. Tests D7, D8.
