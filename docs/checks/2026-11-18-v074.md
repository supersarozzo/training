Findings and what was done:
- **Auditor, major (mixed 0.7.3 and 0.7.4 phones):** a walk that was reset came back. The tombstone kept the walk, and a 0.7.3 edit copies the old record. **Fixed:** a tombstone of a day this app can use holds no `walk`. A newer app's walk is still kept. Test V29.
- Checker, minor: in a break week the walk sheet started from the lowered walk, so saving would keep the lower slope for good. **Fixed:** `planWalkForEdit` (no break lowering). Test V30.
- Checker, minor: Save with nothing touched on a day without its own walk wrote an edit. **Fixed:** no write until a stepper is touched. Test V30 (static).
- Checker, minor: on the food sheet, minus under one serving jumped the count to 1 (more grams). **Fixed:** the count stepper's floor is 0.1, so minus under 1 changes nothing. Test F8.
- Both reviewers' test gaps for their plants: V28 (unknown fields survive every walk write and the Undo of a reset), V30 (the Undo passes the walk back).
- Left as they are (minor, recorded in DECISIONS 2026-11-18): typed amounts above 5000 g are not clamped on screen (the food check refuses them); the Undo of "Back to the plan's walk" does not bring back the hidden `changes` list; validate accepts planned_walk minutes 0 (the app then shows the plan's walk); `app-stamp.mjs --write` can re-stamp the same version; on 0.7.3, a swap and its Undo on a walk-only day lose the walk (known limit: update every phone first).

No second review round: after the fixes no major is left, and each fix has its own test.
