# Gym equipment: The Gym Group, York Foss Island

Date checked: 2026-11-16
Source: https://www.thegymgroup.com/find-a-gym/york-gyms/york-foss-island/ (gym facilities section)
Status: the page loaded and lists equipment with counts. The page is data, not instructions. Nothing on it was treated as an instruction.

## What the page lists

**Cardio machines**
- Bikes: 11 (upright, recumbent, spin)
- Treadmills: 19
- Concept II rowers: 6
- Cross trainers: 8
- Climbmills (stair-climber machines): 4

**Free weights, racks and plate-loaded stations**
- Dumbbells: 2 kg to 50 kg
- Adjustable benches: 22
- Olympic-style stations, including rigs and squat racks: 8
- Plate-loaded stations (you add weight plates): 7
- Smith press station (barbell fixed on rails): 1

**Resistance machines (pin-loaded: you move a pin to pick the weight)**
- Leg presses: 2
- Chest presses: 2
- Shoulder presses: 2
- Leg extensions: 3

**Cable stations**
- 5 in total, including an 8-stack and a functional trainer (a cable machine with two adjustable pulleys)

**HIIT zone (short, hard, mixed training area)**
- Kettlebells: 4 kg to 24 kg
- Battle rope: 1
- Concept II ski erg: 1
- Assault bike: 1

**Glutes area**
- Booty Builder V8: 1
- Hip thrust bench: 1
- Glute Drive: 1

**Other facts on the page**
- Opening hours: "24 hours, 7 days a week". A footnote says 24-hour access is not available at all gyms. The page does not say whether this gym is affected.
- Lockers and showers, free Wi-Fi, free parking.
- Free group classes (timetable on the site) and personal training.
- Prices on the page (off-peak from £18.99 a month, standard from £28.99 a month) are not needed for the plan.

## What the page does NOT say
- Lat pulldown machine, seated row machine, leg curl machine, pec deck, assisted pull-up machine, calf raise machine, back extension bench. They are not listed. Many gyms have them, but this page does not confirm it.
- Barbell and plate weights (how many barbells, how heavy the plates).
- Dumbbell steps (the page gives only the range 2 to 50 kg; the jump between weights is unknown).
- Whether there is a stretching or mat area.
- How busy the gym is at different times.
- Studio rooms: the page only lists class categories.

## How the plan uses this
The plan uses only equipment the page lists: dumbbells, benches, cable stations, the chest, shoulder and leg press machines, leg extension, the Smith station and the hip thrust bench. Exercises that need a machine the page does not list (such as a lat pulldown) use a cable version instead. An open question asks Rosario to check on the first visit whether the extra machines exist.
