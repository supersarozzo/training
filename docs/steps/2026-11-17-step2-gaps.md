## 8. Decisions made in this step (small, no question needed)

- **Rest days on the Plan tab:** on Saturday and Sunday the page opens on the coming Monday with the coming week's sets
  (the plan is about what comes next). Before the block: Monday of week 1. After the 12 weeks: Monday of week 12.
- **Read-only step:** Edit, Swap and Remove are not drawn yet (no dead buttons); they come with steps 3 and 4.
- **"Whole plan check"** opens a short sheet that says the screen comes in build step 5.
- **Plan page memory:** leaving the Plan tab and coming back opens today again (as the spec says "opened on today").
- **Abs headers:** each abs exercise gets its own header with its own rest ("Abs · rest 30 s between sets"), as the
  0.4.1 rows showed each rest.
- **"Day so far" steps:** under 3 sets = A little, 3 to under 6 = More, 6 or more = Most (the mock-up's numbers:
  4.5 and 3 are "More", 1.5 is "A little"); helpers count half.
- **Day arrows across weeks** (added 2026-11-18 after the auditor's finding): Friday's "Monday ›" opens next week's
  Monday, and Monday's "‹ Friday" opens last week's Friday; the page then shows "Showing week 4. This week is week 3.".
  Only at the block's two ends (Monday of week 1, Friday of week 12) do the arrows wrap inside the week.
- **Today ring** on the day strip only when this week is shown (a picked week never marks a day as today).
